# SAKRA VISION — Official Brand & Company Identity

## Company Overview

**SAKRA VISION** is an AI Product Studio and AI Innovation Company established in 2026 by **Likith Naidu Anumakonda**.

**Tagline:** From Ideas to Intelligent Systems.

SAKRA VISION is a student-founded AI product studio and AI innovation company based in India. It focuses on building real-world intelligent systems and innovative technology products using Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), AI Agents, Automation, and Full-Stack Web Technologies.

The company aims to transform ideas into practical, intelligent, scalable, and impactful technology solutions.
