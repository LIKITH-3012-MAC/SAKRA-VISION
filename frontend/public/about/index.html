About SAKRA VISION

SAKRA VISION is an AI product studio established in 2026 by Likith Naidu Anumakonda. The studio builds practical intelligent systems using artificial intelligence, machine learning, computer vision, LLMs, RAG, AI agents, automation and full-stack web technologies.

From ideas to intelligent systems

The engineering work described by SAKRA VISION spans computer-vision scanning and verification pipelines, local LLM implementations, API design, secure databases and reactive user interfaces. The goal is to turn useful concepts into deployable software products.

Our mission

To build intelligent, accessible and impact-driven AI products that help businesses, researchers and public communities address complex challenges through custom engineering and automation.

Our vision

To grow as an AI innovation laboratory and product studio, building reliable products from India for the global deep-tech ecosystem.

How we think

Practical intelligence

Focus on functional, usable AI capabilities and systems that can be evaluated in real workflows.

Accelerated automation

Design agentic software and workflow automation to reduce repetitive operational work.
